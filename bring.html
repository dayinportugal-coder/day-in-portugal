Porto · to take home

Four things worth the weight. The rest is a keyring.

  • A bottle from the cellar you actually visited. Buy it at the end of the tasting, not from a riverside stall with a plastic cork.
  • Tinned fish — sardines, mackerel, octopus. Conservas are the honest souvenir. A good grocer in Cedofeita beats the shop with the giant sardine on the door.
  • Soap from Claus Porto if you want something that lasts after the wine is gone. Their shop is in the centre; the airport also sells it, worse value.
  • One tile, not a set. Azulejos as a single piece travel. A “hand-painted” stack on Rua das Flores is often a mould.

We do not run a shop. Cork bags, rooster magnets and “I love Porto” shirts are fine if they make you smile — they are not why you came.

If you want the same brands shipped home, Amazon and the producers’ own sites sell Claus Porto and canned fish. We do not add a margin on that. When an affiliate programme for those products is approved, the button will say so.
