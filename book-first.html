Porto trip planning

Book these five things for Porto. Then stop shopping.

  1. Hotel — coast or centre. Póvoa / Vila do Conde base · where to sleep in Porto.
  2. One Gaia cellar tasting — timed entry in summer. What to book in Porto.
  3. Douro Valley or not — tour or train, not both. Compare.
  4. Car or airport transfer — after metro vs car. Airport options. Landing late with bags: a pre-booked driver (Welcome Pickups or Kiwitaxi).
  5. First dinner near the hotel — Friday–Saturday in peak months.

Flights not booked yet? Compare Porto (OPO) on Aviasales. Tile shops and sunset spots can wait until you are in Porto.

Two small things before you fly

  • Data on landing. An eSIM installed at home means maps and the metro app work at the gate. Portugal eSIM plans on Airalo, Saily or Yesim — check your phone is unlocked and eSIM-capable first.
  • Travel insurance. Only if your card or home policy does not already cover medical care and cancellation abroad (EU residents: bring the EHIC card too). Two we would look at: Heymondo for a single trip, SafetyWing if you travel for months. Read what is excluded before you buy.
