Porto trip planning

Book these five things for Porto. Then stop shopping.

  1. Hotel — coast or centre. Póvoa / Vila do Conde base.
  2. One Gaia cellar tasting — timed entry in summer.
  3. Douro Valley or not — tour or train, not both. Compare.
  4. Car or airport transfer — after metro vs car.
  5. First dinner near the hotel — Friday–Saturday in peak months.

Tile shops and sunset spots can wait until you are in Porto.
