Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS)

From Lisbon airport to your bed, without a taxi you do not need.

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

The airport station is on the red line, inside the walk from Terminal 1 arrivals. For most people that is the whole transfer. A pre-booked car earns its price late at night, with children, or with more bags than hands.

Three ways in

Metro, taxi, pre-booked car

1. Metro, red line. Metropolitano de Lisboa’s airport page times Aeroporto to Saldanha (it calls Saldanha the centre) at 18 minutes and €1.92. A taxi, without traffic, is 20–30 minutes and €16–19. The page says every journey time is approximate. Checked 6 November 2026 on metrolisboa.pt. Check before you go.

From arrivals at Terminal 1, follow the metro signs. The same page says a contactless bank card, phone or watch works on the validator, so you do not have to buy a paper ticket first. Saldanha is the stop if you are sleeping on Avenida. For Baixa-Chiado, change to the green line. Oriente, for the Évora train, is also on the red line, before Saldanha.

2. Taxi from the rank. The €16–19 figure above is the metro company’s comparison for a ride without traffic, not a quote from a driver. Ask before the bags go in the boot.

3. Pre-booked transfer. A fixed price and a name at arrivals. The calm choice when the metro is a bad idea.

Questions people ask

How do I get from Lisbon airport to the centre?

Red-line metro from the Aeroporto station to Saldanha, about 18 minutes and €1.92 on Metropolitano de Lisboa’s airport page, checked 6 November 2026. Change lines for Baixa-Chiado. Times on that page are approximate.

When is a pre-booked car worth it?

A late landing, children, or more bags than you can carry onto a metro. Otherwise the red line is the transfer.

Should I rent a car at the airport?

If you are leaving Lisbon, not if you are staying in it. Pena and the Moorish Castle do not allow private cars on the access roads.
