From Porto · one day out

Day trips from Porto. Pick one, maybe two.

Northern Portugal is compact: most of these are an hour or two away. With three or four days in Porto, one day out is plenty. Choose by what you came for — wine, old towns, the sea, or a walk.

Aveiro and Costa Nova — canals and striped houses

South of Porto: canals with painted moliceiro boats, art nouveau façades, then the striped beach houses of Costa Nova. Flat and easy — a good day with children or after the Douro. Full guide: Aveiro and Costa Nova day trip.
