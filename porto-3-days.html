Porto itinerary · 3 days

3 days in Porto: Gaia first, centre second, Douro or coast last.

Walking itinerary if you sleep in Porto; metro itinerary if you base in Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde.

Day 1 in Porto — Vila Nova de Gaia and the river

  1. Walk the Gaia riverfront first. The postcard view of Ribeira is free from this side.
  2. Book one port wine cellar with a tasting. Walk-ins in July–September often mean an hour in a queue.
  3. Eat off the front row of Ribeira unless you want to pay twice for the same view.
Book a Gaia cellar and tasting →

Day 2 in Porto — historic centre, one hill, one market

  1. São Bento station hall: fifteen minutes, then leave.
  2. Clérigos, Cedofeita or Miguel Bombarda if you want streets that still work as a city.
  3. Skip Livraria Lello unless the ticket is already bought.

Day 3 — Douro Valley or the coast, not both

Choose a Douro day trip from Porto or a slow day on the Póvoa / Vila do Conde seafront.

Porto bookings that are worth it in high season

  • Timed Gaia tasting
  • One small-group walking tour if you hate maps
  • Six-bridges river cruise only for the city photo — not a Douro Valley day
