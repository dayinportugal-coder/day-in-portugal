Porto itinerary · 3 days

3 days in Porto: Gaia first, centre second, Douro or coast last.

Walking itinerary if you sleep in Porto; metro itinerary if you base in Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde.

Day 1 in Porto — Vila Nova de Gaia and the river

  1. Walk the Gaia riverfront first. The postcard view of Ribeira is free from this side.
  2. Book one port wine cellar with a tasting. Walk-ins in July–September often mean an hour in a queue.
  3. Eat off the front row of Ribeira unless you want to pay twice for the same view.

Day 2 in Porto — historic centre, one hill, one market

  1. São Bento station hall: fifteen minutes, then leave.
  2. Clérigos, Cedofeita or Miguel Bombarda if you want streets that still work as a city.
  3. Skip Livraria Lello unless the ticket is already bought — how Lello tickets work.

If you do want the two queues of the day, buy the timed ticket before you walk over: Livraria Lello tickets · Clérigos Tower tickets.

Day 3 — Douro Valley or the coast, not both

Choose a Douro day trip from Porto or a slow day on the Póvoa / Vila do Conde seafront. If you go upriver and nobody wants to drive, book the tour the day before at the latest. Other options: day trips from Porto.

Where to sleep for three nights

Cedofeita or Bonfim if you walk everywhere; Gaia for the view; Póvoa or Vila do Conde if you want the beach and the metro. More in where to sleep in Porto.

Arriving

Flights: compare Porto (OPO) on Aviasales. Details in Porto airport to the city. From the airport the metro (line E, violet) reaches the centre in about half an hour. Late flight, children or heavy bags: a pre-booked transfer or a fixed-price Kiwitaxi car. Flying out late on the last day? Check out, then leave the bags with Radical Storage in the centre. Mobile data before you land: Portugal eSIM on Saily or Yesim.

Porto bookings that are worth it in high season

  • Timed Gaia tasting
  • One small-group walking tour if you hate maps — or a self-guided audio walk on your phone: Porto audio tours on WeGoTrip
  • Six-bridges river cruise only for the city photo — not a Douro Valley day
