FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, up to 10 certificates per batch and 5 batches a day, with a small "Made with Netmend" line at the bottom.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited names per batch and batches, your logo and signature image, a custom accent colour, and no Netmend line.

Which characters are supported?

Latin alphabets with accents (Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and so on). Non-Latin scripts are not supported in this version.

Can it email the certificates?

No, on purpose: nothing leaves your browser. Download the ZIP and send them from your own email or LMS.
