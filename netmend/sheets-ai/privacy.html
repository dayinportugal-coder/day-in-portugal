Netmend Sheets AI · Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Netmend Sheets AI website and the Google Sheets add-on, published by Netmend (Pedro Carvalho). It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Who we are

Netmend is a project of Pedro Carvalho. Contact: dayinportugal@gmail.com.

What the add-on can access

The Google Sheets add-on asks only for access to the spreadsheet you have open (spreadsheets.currentonly), permission to show its sidebar, and permission to call two external services (see below). It does not ask to see other files in your Google account, your email or your contacts.

When you run a formula, use Fill selected cells or use Bulk fill, the add-on reads the cells you included in that action and may write results into cells you chose.

Where your data goes

  • Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). The prompt and the cell values you include are sent from your Google account, with the Gemini API key you supply, so the model can return an answer. Google processes that request under the Gemini API terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products; do not put confidential material in formulas on the free tier. Netmend never receives this content.
  • Gumroad licence API (api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify), only if you activate Pro: your licence key and our product ID, on activation and about once a week afterwards. Purchases are handled by Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy.

Netmend has no server for this product and does not collect, store, sell or share your spreadsheet content, your Gemini key or your licence key.

What stays in your Google account

  • Your Gemini API key.
  • Your Pro licence key and the date it was last checked.
  • A daily count of free AI calls (date and number only).

These are stored in Apps Script user properties for this add-on, in your Google account. Removing the add-on, or using Remove key / Remove licence in Settings, deletes what the add-on saved.

The website and the paste-in script

The marketing site and the optional paste-in script at this address are static pages or code you paste into your own Apps Script project. The in-browser demo, if you use it, sends the text you type to Google's Gemini API with your key, from your browser. Files are not uploaded to Netmend.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).

Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, processing that happens on our side is limited to answering support email you send us, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support or of the contract for a Pro licence. You can ask to access, correct or delete that correspondence, or complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD). Spreadsheet data is processed in your Google account and by Google, not by us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
