Create the Google Form in 1 minute

  1. Open script.google.com → New project (signed in to your Google account).
  2. Replace the code with the script below (it is already copied to your clipboard) and click Run. Approve the Forms permission (it is your own script).
  3. Open Execution log: it shows the edit link and the link to share with students. Answers are graded automatically.

FAQ


Is it free?
Yes: 3 quizzes a day, up to 10 questions, Google Forms, CSV and print exports. You use your own free Gemini key.


Does Netmend get access to my Google account?
No. You run the generated script yourself in your own Apps Script project. We never see your account, your form or your students' answers.


What does Pro add?
No daily limit, up to 50 questions, Kahoot spreadsheet and Moodle GIFT exports, and no Netmend line on printouts and forms.


Can the AI get an answer wrong?
Sometimes. Each question shows the answer and why, so review the quiz before you use it.
