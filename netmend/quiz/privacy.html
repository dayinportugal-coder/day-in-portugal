Netmend Quiz · Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the Netmend Quiz website and the Google Forms add-on, published by Netmend (Pedro Carvalho). It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Who we are

Netmend is a project of Pedro Carvalho. Contact: dayinportugal@gmail.com.

What the add-on can access

The Google Forms add-on asks only for access to the form you have open (forms.currentonly), permission to show its sidebar, and permission to call two external services (see below). It does not create a new form and does not ask to see other forms, your Drive, your email or your contacts.

When you add a quiz, the add-on writes questions, answers, points and feedback into that open form. Student replies are stored in your Google account; the add-on does not read responses unless you later use the form yourself in Google Forms.

Where your data goes

  • Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). The lesson, topic or text you paste, and your instructions (language, number of questions, types), are sent from your Google account, with the Gemini API key you supply, so the model can draft questions. Google processes that request under the Gemini API terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its products; do not paste confidential pupil or client material on the free tier. Netmend never receives this content.
  • Gumroad licence API (api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify), only if you activate Pro: your licence key and our product ID, on activation and about once a week afterwards. Purchases are handled by Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy.

Netmend has no server for this product and does not collect, store, sell or share your lesson text, your form, your students' answers, your Gemini key or your licence key.

What stays in your Google account

  • Your Gemini API key.
  • Your Pro licence key and the date it was last checked.
  • A daily count of free quizzes (date and number only).
  • The last generated quiz, so you can add it to the form after you review it.

These are stored in Apps Script user properties for this add-on, in your Google account. Removing the add-on, or using Remove key / Remove licence in Settings, deletes what the add-on saved.

The website and the paste-in script

The website is a static page. Files you choose are read inside your browser. Extracted text and your instructions are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API with the key you supply. The Google Forms export on the website gives you an Apps Script that you run in your own Google account; Netmend gets no access to that account. Printed tests, CSV, Kahoot and Moodle files are generated in your browser.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security (GitHub privacy statement).

Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, processing that happens on our side is limited to answering support email you send us, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support or of the contract for a Pro licence. You can ask to access, correct or delete that correspondence, or complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD). Form and pupil data is processed in your Google account and, for generation, by Google, not by us. You are the controller of any pupil data you put in a form.

Changes
