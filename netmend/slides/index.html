FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes: 3 decks a day of up to 8 slides, with a small "Made with Netmend Slides" line. You use your own free Gemini API key from Google AI Studio, so we have no AI bill to pass on to you.

Can I edit the slides?

Yes. The download is a standard .pptx with normal text boxes. Upload it to Google Slides (File, Import) or open it in PowerPoint or Keynote.

Where do my PDF and text go?

From your browser straight to Google's Gemini API, using your key. This site is static: there is no Netmend server, so we never see your files.

What does Pro add?

No daily limit, up to 30 slides, no Netmend branding, and a custom accent colour. One payment through Gumroad, 14-day refund.

Which sources work best?

Reports, articles, lesson notes and product docs. For web pages, the page must be public. Scanned image-only PDFs work too, since Gemini reads the pages.
