FAQ

How does it read the video?

Your browser asks Google's Gemini API, with your key, to watch the public YouTube video directly (Gemini supports YouTube links). Long videos take longer; a 20-minute video usually takes under a minute. Private and unlisted videos do not work: paste the transcript instead.

Is it free?

3 videos a day and 5 questions per video, with your own free Gemini key. Google's free tier has its own daily video limits.

Do you store my videos or questions?

No. There is no Netmend server. Your notes stay in this tab until you close it.

What does Pro add?

No daily limit, unlimited questions, blog post / X thread / LinkedIn / study-notes generation, and Markdown export.
