Three bookings. Then stop shopping.

Each button opens the public page on GetYourGuide or Viator, already filtered to Porto. You pay their price. If a booking completes through our partner link, we may earn a commission — usually about 8% from them, not added to you.

1. One Gaia cellar, timed

Cross the Dom Luís I bridge. Do one tasting with a slot, not three. Sandeman, Taylor’s and Cálem are the names you will see; pick by the time that fits your afternoon, not the logo.

2. Six bridges, not the valley

The short boat under the bridges is the city photo. It is about 50 minutes. It is not a day in the wine valley. If you only have three days, this is the one boat.

3. A walk, if you want a person

São Bento, the river, one hill. A small group is worth it if you would rather not look at a map. Skip Livraria Lello unless the timed ticket is already in your pocket.
