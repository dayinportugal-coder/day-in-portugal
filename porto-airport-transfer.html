Day in Portugal / Porto airport to the city

Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO)

From Porto airport to your bed, without overthinking it.

Porto’s airport is close and on the metro. For most people the metro is the answer. A pre-booked driver earns its price late at night, with children, or with more bags than hands.

Four ways in

Metro, taxi, transfer, car

1. Metro, line E (violet). From the station at arrivals to Trindade in about half an hour; change there for São Bento or Gaia. Buy a rechargeable Andante card at the machines. It does not run all night — check the last train if you land late.

2. To the coast. For Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde, take line E and change at Verdes to line B (red).

3. Taxi or ride-hailing. From the rank or pick-up zone outside arrivals. Fine for two people with luggage.

4. Pre-booked transfer. A driver with your name in arrivals, a fixed price agreed before you fly. The calm choice for a late landing, a family, or a group.
