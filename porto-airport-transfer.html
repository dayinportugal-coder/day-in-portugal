Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO)

Porto airport to the city centre: metro, taxi or transfer

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

Porto’s airport is close and on the metro. For most people the metro is the answer. A pre-booked driver earns its price late at night, with children, or with more bags than hands.

Quick answer

  • Metro line E (violet): airport to Trindade in about 27 minutes, every 15 minutes 07:00–20:00, first 06:06, last 00:42. Z4 ticket €2.30 plus €0.60 for the rechargeable Andante card, or tap a contactless bank card at the marked validators (Metro do Porto 2026 prices).
  • Taxi or Uber: from outside arrivals, door to door — simplest for two or three people with bags.
  • Pre-booked private transfer: a driver with your name, a fixed price, flight tracking — the calm option late at night, with children or a group.

Four ways in

Metro, taxi, transfer, car

1. Metro, line E (violet). From the station at arrivals to Trindade in about half an hour; change there for São Bento or Gaia. Buy a rechargeable Andante card at the machines. It does not run all night — check the last train if you land late.

2. To the coast. For Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde, take line E and change at Verdes to line B (red).

3. Taxi or ride-hailing. From the rank or pick-up zone outside arrivals. Fine for two people with luggage.

4. Pre-booked transfer. A driver with your name in arrivals, a fixed price agreed before you fly. The calm choice for a late landing, a family, or a group.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get from Porto airport to the city centre?

Take metro line E (violet) from the Aeroporto station to Trindade, about 27 minutes; change there for São Bento or Gaia. A taxi, Uber or a pre-booked transfer goes door to door.

How much is the metro from Porto airport?

A Z4 single costs €2.30 in 2026, plus €0.60 for the rechargeable Andante card if you do not have one (Metro do Porto price table, in force from 1 January 2026). On line E you can also pay with a contactless bank card at marked validators.

Is a private transfer from Porto airport worth it?

When you land late, travel with children or heavy luggage, or are three or more people splitting one fixed price. Otherwise the metro is quick and cheap.

What time is the last metro from Porto airport?

00:42; the first one leaves at 06:06 (Metro do Porto frequencies valid from 07/09/2026, checked 13/11/2026). After that, use a taxi or a pre-booked transfer.

How often does the airport metro run?

Every 15 minutes from 07:00 to 20:00, every day; every 20–30 minutes early morning and late evening (Metro do Porto, checked 13/11/2026).

How do I get from Porto airport to Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde?

Take metro line E and change at Verdes to line B (red), or book a transfer straight to the door.
