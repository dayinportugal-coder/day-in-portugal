Opening a bank account in Portugal as a foreigner

Most landlords, employers and consulates will eventually ask you to show a Portuguese account or funds available in Portugal. The official starting points are a NIF and, if you qualify, the Banco de Portugal rules on minimum banking services.

Get the NIF first

A Portuguese tax number (NIF) is the identifier banks use. Official application routes are on gov.pt, the Portal das Finanças (English) and the gov.pt guide for foreigners. Our walk-through is the NIF and NISS page. Some people can ask for a NIF, a NISS and a health-user number in one visit at selected Espaços Cidadão, if they already have a Portuguese address and meet the conditions on this gov.pt service.

Minimum banking services, in official terms

Banco de Portugal’s Portal do Cliente Bancário explains the serviços mínimos bancários account: a current account with a legally capped fee, for people who meet the conditions in the law (including, as a rule, not already holding another current account). The official pages describe the documents typically involved (identification, and often a tax number and address proof) and a decision deadline of 10 working days for a complete request. Read “O que são” and the brochure on the same portal rather than a bank’s marketing page.

Banks remain responsible for customer due diligence. They can ask for more documents, and they can refuse an application that does not meet their legal duties. This page does not rank banks and does not claim that any particular bank will open an account for visa applicants before they arrive.

How this meets a visa file

D7 means must be secured for at least 12 months and, for a pension or passive income, shown to be received or available in Portugal (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, art. 5.º(6); MNE: means of subsistence). That is not automatically the same thing as “deposit 12 × €920 in a Portuguese account”. Follow the checklist of the consulate that will decide your file, and use the income calculator for the arithmetic.

Quick answers

Do I need a NIF to open a Portuguese bank account?

In practice, yes: banks identify customers for tax purposes with a Portuguese NIF. Apply through the official routes on gov.pt and Portal das Finanças first. Having a NIF does not, by itself, entitle you to an account.

What is the minimum banking services account?

Banco de Portugal describes a basic current account (serviços mínimos bancários) with a legal cap on fees, for people who meet the statutory conditions and do not already hold another current account, with limited exceptions. Official pages: Portal do Cliente Bancário. The bank must accept or refuse a complete request within a maximum of 10 working days.

Do I need the account before I apply for a D7 or D8?

The legal texts on means of subsistence talk about income being received or available in Portugal; they do not, by themselves, always require a Portuguese account holding 12 months of the minimum wage when regular income is shown. Your consulate’s checklist is the one that counts. See the D7 guide and the income calculator for the wording we have checked.
