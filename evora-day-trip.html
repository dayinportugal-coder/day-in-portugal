From Lisbon · the Alentejo

Évora in a day: one Intercidades out, one back, and the temple from the square.

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

Évora is not a metro ride. CP runs a handful of Intercidades trains, the seat is reserved, and the useful ones are in the morning out and the late afternoon back. If your date is not on the sheet, do not invent a midday departure. Go another day, or stay in Lisbon.

The trains that make a day

The CP sheet “Intercidades / Regional, Linha do Alentejo”, in force since 6 September 2026, lists through trains from Lisboa Oriente to Évora. Two morning examples: 07:02 from Oriente, 08:47 into Évora (1 hour 45); 09:02 from Oriente, 10:45 into Évora (1 hour 43). The same trains call at Entrecampos and Sete Rios a few minutes after Oriente — 07:10 and 07:14 on the 07:02, 09:10 and 09:14 on the 09:02 — so you do not have to start at the airport station.

Coming back, that sheet lists departures from Évora at 07:00, 09:14, 11:30, 16:51 and 19:14. A day in town is a morning train out and the 16:51 or the 19:14 back. The 11:30 is too early if you only arrived at 10:45.

The remarks column matters more than the clock. CP marks some trains Monday–Friday except public holidays, and some Saturday, Sunday and public holidays. The PDF is comboios Lisboa–Évora–Beja. Buy the seat on cp.pt. There is no walk-up urban train every 20 minutes on this line. Check before you go, including the fare, which is on the booking page and not repeated here.

What the day is for

The walled centre is a UNESCO World Heritage site, and it is walkable from the station without a taxi for the temple and the squares. The Roman temple — often called the Temple of Diana — stands in the open. You see it from the square. You do not need a ticket to stand there.

The cathedral is the paid interior, up the hill from the temple. The church of São Francisco keeps the Capela dos Ossos, the chapel lined with bones. Both charge at the door; the price is on the desk that day, so check it there rather than from a blog. If you only have the hours between 10:45 and 16:51, do the square, the temple, one church, lunch, and the walk back to the station. A second village is how you miss the train.

Train, unless the day is bigger than Évora

Questions people ask

How long is the train from Lisbon to Évora?

About 1 hour 40 to 1 hour 45 on the Intercidades. The CP timetable since 6 September 2026 times 07:02 from Lisboa Oriente to 08:47 in Évora, and 09:02 to 10:45. Not every train runs every day.

Do you need a car?

Not for the centre. You need a reserved seat on the right train, and a return that still exists on your date.

Is the Roman temple free?

It stands in a public square. You look at it without a ticket. The cathedral and the Capela dos Ossos are indoors and charged.
