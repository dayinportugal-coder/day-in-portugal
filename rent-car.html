Day in Portugal / Car or metro?

Porto airport OPO · Metro do Porto

Most Porto trips do not need a hire car.

Presentes de Portugal / Gifts from Portugal — ver loja

The historic centre is hills, one-ways and paid parking. Metro do Porto plus walking covers Gaia and the centre.

Do not rent a car in Porto if

  • You only do Porto, Gaia and one organised Douro tour.
  • You sleep in Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde and use the red line.
  • Nobody wants the N222 behind coaches.

Rent at Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO) if

  • Two or more adults, your own Douro viewpoints, luggage, children.
  • You land late and go straight to the coast.
  • You add Peneda-Gerês or a quinta off the train line.

Just getting from the plane to the hotel? See Porto airport to the city.

If you do rent

Compare Porto airport (OPO) and read the insurance excess before you pay. Maps need data on the N222: a Portugal eSIM on Saily (or Yesim) is cheaper than roaming for most non-EU phones.
