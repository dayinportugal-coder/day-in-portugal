Port wine cellars in Gaia: which cellar tour to book

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

The lodges sit across the river from Ribeira, a walk over the lower deck of the Dom Luís I bridge. They all explain the same process — grapes from the Douro, ageing in Gaia — so choose by the kind of visit, not the brand. In summer, book a time; walk-ins wait.

Quick answer

  • First time, no plan: Taylor’s — self-paced audio tour in 13 languages and three ports, €25 (ages 8–17 €9), open daily 10:00–18:15 (Taylor’s FAQ, checked 13/11/2026).
  • Guided and by the river: Cálem — museum, cellar and tasting in about an hour; an evening version adds fado.
  • Cheapest self-paced: Fonseca, €16 with three ports (children 5–14 €8). Working lodge: Cockburn’s, about 1½ hours, reservation only (official sites, checked 13/11/2026).

Frequently asked questions

Which port wine cellar in Gaia is best?

For a first visit, Taylor’s (self-paced, three ports, garden view) or Cálem (guided, on the riverfront). For a quieter, smaller visit try Burmester, Fonseca or the family-run Poças.

How much does a port cellar tour cost in Gaia?

Taylor’s costs €25 for adults with three ports and €9 for ages 8 to 17, under 8 free (Taylor’s FAQ, checked 13/11/2026). Fonseca’s standard visit is €16, children 5–14 €8 (Fonseca, checked 13/11/2026). Tastings of older ports cost more.

Do I need to book a port cellar visit in advance?

At Cockburn’s, yes — reservation only. Elsewhere a time slot avoids waiting in summer and at weekends; Taylor’s also sells on site, subject to availability.

What are the opening hours of the port cellars?

Taylor’s cellar 10:00–18:15 daily; Fonseca last audio-tour admission 17:45 (opens 11:00); Cockburn’s 10:00–17:00 from November to March (official sites, checked 13/11/2026). Special dates vary.

How many port cellars should I visit?

One, maybe two. They all explain the same process, and after the second tasting they taste the same. Do one cellar, then eat.

How do I get to the cellars from Porto?

Walk across the lower deck of the Dom Luís I bridge from Ribeira; the lodges are on the Gaia riverfront and the lanes above it. The Gaia cable car runs back up to the top of the bridge.
