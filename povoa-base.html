Póvoa de Varzim · Vila do Conde · Metro do Porto

Stay in Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde. Day-trip Porto by metro.

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

Both towns sit on the Atlantic and on Metro do Porto’s red line. You get a beach evening and, on most dates, a cheaper room than Ribeira. You still do São Bento, the Gaia cellars and the river in a normal city day.

Who should sleep on the coast

  • Couples or families who want sea air after Porto’s hills.
  • Anyone comparing Ribeira hotel prices and flinching.
  • Stay in central Porto instead if you want bars until 3am every night.

Metro do Porto: airport, coast, city

  • Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport → coast: metro toward Póvoa de Varzim. Pre-book a transfer only if you land late with heavy bags.
  • Póvoa / Vila do Conde → Porto: red line. Get off around Trindade, Bolhão or São Bento and walk.
  • Last trains: not 24h. Check before a late dinner in town.

Day trips from the coast

All the options are on day trips from Porto; these are the easiest from Póvoa.

  • Braga and Guimarães. Both are under an hour by car from Póvoa. Trains run from Porto to each; one guided day from Porto does both: Braga & Guimarães day trip.
  • Douro Valley. Train, tour or car — compared on the Douro day trip page. The classic Douro day with lunch.
  • By car. From the coast a hire car makes more sense than from the centre: the A28 runs north and south, and you skip Porto’s centre entirely. Compare car hire in Porto · local rental firms on Localrent.

GetYourGuide · Auto Europe · Localrent

On the coast itself the seafront is flat and easy on two wheels. To hire a bike or scooter, compare on Bikesbooking.

Trade-off. About 35 minutes each way, usually less noise and a lower room rate than Ribeira.
