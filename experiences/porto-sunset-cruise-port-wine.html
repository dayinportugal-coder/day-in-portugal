What’s included

As listed on the GetYourGuide product page (“Porto: 7 Bridges Cruise & Port Wine with Sunset Option”) on 15 Nov 2026.

Good to know

  • Afurada is on the Gaia side near the river mouth — allow time to get there by taxi or by the small ferry from Cantareira.
  • Blankets are on board, but bring a layer: the river is cold after sunset.
  • Eat grilled fish in Afurada before or after.
