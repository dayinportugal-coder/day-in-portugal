Póvoa de Varzim

Beach base: Póvoa and Vila do Conde

Póvoa de Varzim and Vila do Conde sit at the end of Porto's metro line B: long Atlantic beaches, a fishing harbour and grilled fish. No tour needed — our free guide has the details.

Duration
About 1 hour by metro (line B)
Where
Metro from Trindade, Porto

Free guide

No ticket needed. Our guide has the trains, times and what to see.

Read the free guide

Good to know

  • The Andante card works on the metro.
  • The Atlantic is cold even in August.
  • Vila do Conde's aqueduct and old town are one stop before Póvoa.
