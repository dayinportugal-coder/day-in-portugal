Moving to Portugal from the USA in 2026

A US passport is enough for a short Schengen visit. It is not enough to live in Portugal. The usual long-stay routes are the D7 and the D8, applied for at a Portuguese consulate in the United States, then completed with AIMA after you arrive.

Start at the consulate, finish at AIMA

National visa types and document lists are published by the MNE. Use the consulate that has jurisdiction over where you live in the United States; the MNE portal is the place to confirm which post that is. The visa is typically valid for four months and two entries. The residence permit is then requested from AIMA.

The 2026 income figures

Mainland minimum wage: €920 (Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025). D7 for one adult: €920 a month and €11,040 over 12 months. D8: €3,680 a month, averaged over the last three months. Compare them in the D7 vs D8 guide and run your household through the calculator.

NIF, housing, tax

Apply for a NIF through Portal das Finanças or gov.pt before you try to sign a lease or open an account. Budget rent from the official INE figures, especially if you are aiming at Lisbon, where the Q2 2026 municipal median was 17.79 €/m².

Tax residency follows article 16 of the IRS Code. US citizenship does not switch that test off. The IFICI regime, which replaced NHR for new residents from 2024, only covers eligible Portuguese employment or self-employment income, and only after a formal registration on the Portal das Finanças. It is not a general 20% rate on US-source income.

Quick answers

Can I move to Portugal from the US without a visa?

No. Visa-free entry covers a short stay in the Schengen area, in principle up to 90 days in any 180-day period, not residence. To live in Portugal you need a national visa from the Portuguese consulate or visa centre that covers the US state where you legally live, and then a residence permit from AIMA.

D7 or D8 for Americans?

It depends on the income. Pensions and passive income usually point to the D7 (€920 a month for one adult in 2026, means for 12 months). Remote work for a US employer or US clients usually points to the D8 (€3,680 a month, averaged over three months).

Does a US tax filing replace Portuguese tax residency?

No. The US taxes its citizens on worldwide income, and Portugal applies its own residency tests in article 16 of the IRS Code. The two systems can apply at the same time. Treaty relief, if any, is individual. The old NHR regime is closed to new residents from 2024; IFICI is narrower and formal.
