Renting in Portugal as a foreigner

A visa file almost always asks for proof of accommodation. A lease that was never reported to the tax authority is a weak proof. Here is the official reporting duty, the official rent statistics, and the documents you will be asked for.

Before you sign

Get a NIF first. Read the contract in a language you understand; the legally relevant text is usually the Portuguese version. Check the term, the deposit, who pays the condominium fee, and whether the property can lawfully be let as a long-term home (as opposed to short-term lodging). We are not a letting agency and we do not inspect properties.

The contract has to be reported to the tax authority

The landlord should communicate the start of the lease to the Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira. Official guidance is in the Portal das Finanças FAQs on stamp duty and rental contracts: the Modelo 2 declaration, as a rule by the end of the month following the start of the contract, after which a stamp-duty charge may be issued where it is due. Ask the landlord for confirmation that the contract was submitted. A visa or AIMA file that asks for proof of accommodation is stronger with a reported contract than with an informal email.

Official rents, not asking prices

In the second quarter of 2026, INE put the median new rent at 10.17 €/m² nationally, 17.79 €/m² in the municipality of Lisbon and 10.83 €/m² in the Porto metropolitan area. A worked example of 70 m² is about €1,245 in Lisbon and about €758 in the Porto metro area; that is a multiplication of the official median, not a quote. Detail: Lisbon, Porto and the national overview.

Accommodation in a visa or AIMA file

Consulates and AIMA ask for proof of housing. AIMA’s own pages on residence and family reunification describe a sworn statement of address and, for tenants, a declaration from the landlord referring to the legal basis for occupying the property (art. 77; family reunification). Take the wording from the official checklist that applies to you, not from this paragraph.

Quick answers

Do I need a NIF to rent in Portugal?

In practice, yes. The landlord has to identify the tenant to the tax authority when the contract is reported, and a Portuguese NIF is the usual identifier. Official NIF routes are on gov.pt and Portal das Finanças.

Who reports the rental contract?

The landlord (senhorio) reports the start of the contract to the Autoridade Tributária, through the Modelo 2 declaration on the Portal das Finanças, as a rule by the end of the month after the contract starts. Stamp duty (imposto do selo) may then be assessed. See the official FAQs on the Portal das Finanças.

What are typical rents in 2026?

INE does not publish a ‘typical two-bedroom’ price. In Q2 2026 the median new-contract rent was 10.17 €/m² nationally, 17.79 €/m² in the municipality of Lisbon and 10.83 €/m² in the Porto metropolitan area (INE, 29 September 2026). Those are signed contracts, not advertisements.
