Healthcare in Portugal for foreigners: SNS, insurance and visas

Two separate questions get mixed up: can you be treated in a public hospital, and will the Serviço Nacional de Saúde pay? Official pages answer both, and consulates still often ask for a private or travel policy in the visa file.

The SNS user number

The official service page, “Obter o número nacional de utente do SNS” (updated 12 May 2026), says that foreigners are given the number the first time they attend a public unit. The health regulator (ERS) explains that the number comes from registration in the Registo Nacional de Utentes, and that several registration statuses exist. Access to care and financial coverage are not the same thing.

People with a Portuguese address who already have, or have applied for, a residence permit — or who are EU citizens or certain protection applicants — may be able to request a NIF, a NISS and the health-user number in one go at selected Espaços Cidadão (gov.pt service, updated 17 September 2026).

When the SNS covers the bill

The official guide “Migrantes: cuidados de saúde em Portugal” states that any foreigner who feels unwell has the right to be seen in a health centre or public hospital. It also states that the user number alone does not guarantee that the SNS pays. For the SNS to cover costs, the registration should also include, in the guide’s wording, an identity document, a Portuguese NIF, a complete address in Portugal and a valid residence permit, with some situations provided for by law where coverage can apply without all of those data.

EU citizens usually use the European Health Insurance Card for a temporary stay, under the rules of their home system. That card is not a substitute for a residence-based registration once you live here, and it is not a visa insurance policy.

Insurance in the visa file

Until AIMA has issued a residence permit and your SNS registration is in order, you should not assume public coverage. Consulates commonly ask for insurance with the national visa documents. Quick answers

How do I get an SNS user number as a foreigner?

gov.pt says the national health-user number is issued the first time a foreigner goes to a public health unit, such as a health centre or hospital. In some cases, people with a Portuguese address can also request the NIF, NISS and health-user number together at selected Espaços Cidadão. Portuguese citizens receive the number automatically with the Cartão de Cidadão.

Does the SNS number mean my care is free or covered?

No. gov.pt is explicit: having the number does not, by itself, mean the SNS will cover the cost. For coverage, the official migrant health guide lists a Portuguese NIF, a full address in Portugal and a valid residence permit among the data that need to be attached to the registration, with legal exceptions.

Do I still need insurance for a D7 or D8 visa?

Often yes. Consulate checklists commonly ask for health or travel insurance. The official visa pages do not name a required insurer. Read your own consulate’s list, and read the policy wording before you buy.
