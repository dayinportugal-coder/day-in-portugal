Porto cost of living 2026: official rents in the metro area

The official rent figure for the second quarter of 2026 is published for the Porto metropolitan area as a whole. It is lower than Lisbon’s municipal median. Here is exactly what INE published, and what it does not say.

What INE published for Porto

In its highlight of 29 September 2026, INE listed the Área Metropolitana do Porto among the five regions with the highest median new rents in the second quarter of 2026, at 10.83 €/m².

Area, Q2 2026Median new rent
Porto metropolitan area (AMP)10.83 €/m²
Portugal10.17 €/m²
Greater Lisbon15.24 €/m²
Lisbon municipality17.79 €/m²
 Source: INE, Estatísticas de Rendas da Habitação ao nível local, 2nd quarter 2026. Medians of signed new contracts, not asking prices. The AMP includes Gaia, Matosinhos, Maia and other councils around the city of Porto.

A worked example, not an official typical rent: 70 m² × 10.83 €/m² ≈ €758 a month at the metro median. The municipality of Porto can be higher than the metro figure; we do not invent a city-only number that the highlight does not quote. If you need the council figure, open the INE tables attached to that highlight.

Next to the 2026 visa figures

The mainland minimum wage, and the D7 floor for one adult, is €920 a month in 2026 (Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025). At the official metro median, a 70 m² worked example sits below that floor, unlike the same exercise for Lisbon. That is arithmetic, not a guarantee that you will find such a lease, and consulates can still ask for more than the legal minimum.

The D8 floor remains €3,680 a month (four times the minimum wage). Run your household through the calculator.

Quick answers

What is the official median rent in Porto in 2026?

INE’s highlight of 29 September 2026 puts the median rent of new contracts in the Área Metropolitana do Porto at 10.83 €/m² in the second quarter of 2026. The national median was 10.17 €/m². The highlight does not quote a separate figure for the municipality of Porto in the summary; municipal tables for the 24 largest councils are published alongside it.

Is Porto cheaper than Lisbon to rent?

On the official Q2 2026 figures, yes, by a wide margin: 10.83 €/m² for the Porto metro area against 17.79 €/m² for the municipality of Lisbon and 15.24 €/m² for Greater Lisbon. The municipality of Porto itself may sit above the metro average; check INE’s municipal tables for the latest council figure.

How does that sit next to the D7 income rule?

The 2026 D7 floor for one adult is €920 a month. A worked example of 70 m² at the official Porto metro median is about €758 a month in rent. That is a multiplication of the official median, not a promised lease, and it does not include bills.
