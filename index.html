Porto · Póvoa de Varzim · Douro Valley

Porto itinerary that uses the metro, the coast and one Douro day.

First-time visitors book Ribeira, overpay, and never see the Atlantic 30–40 minutes north. This guide is for a 3–5 day trip: sleep in Póvoa de Varzim or Vila do Conde, ride Metro do Porto into the historic centre, visit one Gaia cellar, and choose train, tour or car for the Douro Valley.
