From Lisbon · Sintra

Sintra in a day: the train is easy, the palace clock is not.

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

Pena, the Moorish Castle and Quinta da Regaleira are three visits, not one stroll. The train from Rossio is the simple part. The part that ruins the day is a palace ticket timed for the door, with a hill still in front of you.

What you are actually choosing

Park and National Palace of Pena. The coloured palace is inside the park, up the hill. Parques de Sintra’s Essential Visit — park plus the palace interior — is €20 for ages 18–64, €18 for ages 6–17 and for over-65s, and €65 for a family of two adults and two youths. Checked 6 November 2026 on the Pena page. The park is listed 09:00–19:00, last admission 18:00. The palace is listed 09:30–18:30, last ticket 17:30 and last admission 18:00. A shuttle inside the park runs about every 15 minutes. Check before you go. Other tickets (park only, guided, a longer interior) are on the same page; the price that matters is the one next to the name you select.

The time on a palace ticket is the time at the palace door, not at the park gate. Parques de Sintra says to allow about 30 minutes between the two, on foot or on the shuttle, and that there is no tolerance if you are late. A missed slot is not refunded. Read that on their ticket conditions before you pick a slot.

Moorish Castle. The walls above the town. Adult €12, youth and senior €10, family (two adults and two youths) €33. Open 09:30–18:00, last entry 17:30. The staffed ticket office closes from 12:00 to 13:00; the machines stay on. Same check, the castle page, 6 November 2026. Private cars are not allowed on the access road.

Quinta da Regaleira. The palace and gardens on the edge of the historic centre, including the initiation well. From January 2026 the foundation’s tariff is €20 for ages 18–64, €15 for ages 6–17 and for over-65s, free under 5, and €60 for a family of two adults or seniors and two youths. In October–December the gate opens at 10:00, last entry is 17:30 and the garden closes at 18:30. April–September it opens at 09:00 and closes at 19:30. Slots are every half hour, with up to one hour of lateness. Closed 1 January and 24, 25 and 31 December. Checked 6 November 2026 on regaleira.pt. The foundation says the only authorised online till is regaleira.byblueticket.pt, and that it does not answer for tickets bought anywhere else.

The train, then the bus — in that order

Urban trains run from Lisboa Rossio to Sintra. Parques de Sintra also lists Oriente and Entrecampos as departure stations. On CP’s urban timetable for Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, the 06:01 from Rossio is timed into Sintra at 06:41 — 40 minutes. Other columns on the same sheet end at Mira Sintra-Meleças, short of Sintra. Read the destination on the board. The sheet is CP’s Lisbon urban timetable. Check it for your date. Tickets are bought at the station, not as a reserved seat.

From Sintra station, Parques de Sintra sends you to Pena and to the Moorish Castle on bus 434 (Scotturb). The operator’s own site, Sintra 434, says the 434 starts every day at 08:50, about every 10 minutes, and that the circuit serves Pena, the Moorish Castle and the National Palace in the centre. The same FAQ gives the last departure from Pena as 19:08 in one answer and 19:00 in the next, and says the service ends at 18:40. Use the page the morning you go. A day ticket for the 434 and the 435 is €13.50, with 8% off online, valid 24 hours from the first validation. It does not include palace or castle entry.

Regaleira is not on the 434. The operator puts it on the 435 (Monserrate circuit), which the same FAQ starts at 09:00. You can also walk over from the historic centre once you are down from the hill.

A day that fits the clock

Book the Pena palace slot late enough that the train, the 434 from 08:50, and the 30 minutes inside the park still land you at the door on time. A slot at 09:30 is how people miss the palace. Late morning is the one that survives a delay.

Do the castle on the way down if the 434 stops there and you still have legs. Leave Regaleira for the afternoon: in November last entry is 17:30, and the official till is the one to use. Two of the three is a good day. All three is a march, and only if Pena was the morning and Regaleira is the last slot you can still make.

A hire car does not solve Sintra

Parques de Sintra says private vehicles are not allowed up to Pena or to the Moorish Castle. You park in the historic centre or in the peripheral car parks and still take the bus or a trail. A car earns its place only if the same day continues to the coast. Pick it up when you leave Lisbon, not for the cobbles: local rental firms in Lisbon on Localrent.

Questions people ask

Can you do Pena, the castle and Regaleira in one day?

The train is about 40 minutes. The palace time is at the door, and the park asks for about 30 minutes before that. The 434 does not start until 08:50. Two of the three is comfortable. All three only works with a late-morning Pena slot and Regaleira at the end, and it will feel like a march.

Do you have to book Pena ahead?

Yes. The palace ticket is a date and a time. Parques de Sintra says there is no delay tolerance, you cannot go in early, and a missed slot is not refunded.

Does the 434 ticket include the monuments?

No. Sintra 434 says the day ticket is the bus. Palace, castle and Regaleira are separate tickets.
