Lisbon trip planning

Book these before a Lisbon trip. Then stop shopping.

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

  1. Pena, if Sintra is in the plan. The palace ticket is a date and a time at the door. Parques de Sintra’s Essential Visit is €20 for ages 18–64, checked 6 November 2026, and a late arrival is not let in. The how and the bus are on the Sintra day. Official till first; or Pena on Tiqets.
  2. Jerónimos cloister, and the tower only if you want the inside. €18 and €15 on the Museus e Monumentos price table, same date, closed Mondays. Hours and the train to Belém: day 2 of the Lisbon itinerary. Jerónimos tickets · Belém Tower tickets.
  3. Oceanário, if that afternoon is really free. Open every day 10:00–20:00, last entry 19:00. The visit ticket on the plan-your-visit page depends on the slot: €29.90 from 10:00–11:30, €27.70 from 12:00–15:30, €25.50 from 16:00–18:30, ages 13–64. Under 2 is free. Checked 6 November 2026. Check before you go. Buy at tickets.oceanario.pt or compare Oceanário tickets on Tiqets.
  4. The bed, in a neighbourhood you can walk home to. Baixa, Chiado, Alfama, Príncipe Real, Saldanha or Belém.
  5. Airport transfer only if the metro is the wrong tool. Red line to Saldanha is about 18 minutes and €1.92 on the metro’s own page. A car is for a late landing or a pile of bags: Kiwitaxi from Lisbon airport. The rest is airport to the city.
  6. The Évora seat, if that day is real. A handful of Intercidades, not a commuter train. Which ones make a day.
  7. Regaleira on the foundation’s till if it is in the Sintra day — regaleira.byblueticket.pt — not a random reseller. The 434 bus ticket does not include it.

Two small things before you fly

  • Data on landing. An eSIM installed at home means the metro map works at the gate. Portugal eSIM plans on Yesim — check the phone is unlocked and eSIM-capable first.
  • Travel insurance. Only if your card or home policy does not already cover medical care and cancellation abroad. EU residents: bring the EHIC card too. EKTA (referral link) is one travel-insurance option. SafetyWing is another name some travellers look at. Read what is excluded before you buy.
