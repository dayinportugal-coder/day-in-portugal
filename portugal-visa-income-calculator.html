Check your numbers

For D7, the ordinance uses the minimum wage net of social-security contributions (not necessarily net of income tax).

Your document checklist

    How it is calculated. D7: first adult 100% of the minimum wage, each other adult 50%, each child under 18 or dependent adult child 30% (Portaria n.º 1563/2007, art. 2.º(2)). Other dependent adults (for example a parent) count as adults, at 50%. The means must be secured for at least 12 months and, for a pension or passive income, shown to be received or available in Portugal (art. 5.º(6)(a)–(b)), so we also show the 12-month total. It does not, by itself, require a Portuguese bank account holding 12 months of the minimum wage when regular income is shown. D8: average monthly income over the last 3 months of at least 4 × the minimum wage (Decreto Regulamentar n.º 84/2007, art. 31.º-A for the residence visa and art. 18.º-B for the temporary-stay visa; added by Decreto Regulamentar n.º 4/2022). No official text says whether family shares sit on top of the D8 figure, so we show both. Minimum wage 2026 (mainland): €920 (Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025). These figures use the mainland minimum wage published by the MNE and by Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025. Whether a higher regional minimum wage applies if you will live in Madeira or the Azores is not confirmed here. Sources: MNE visa portal: means of subsistence, MNE: residence visa documents, DGERT: minimum wage 2026.
