Lisbon cost of living 2026: what official rents actually show

Lisbon is the most expensive large municipality in Portugal to rent, according to the official figures for the second quarter of 2026. Here is what INE actually published, and how that sits next to the 2026 visa income rules.

The official Lisbon figures, Q2 2026

Statistics Portugal (INE, 29 September 2026) reported:

AreaMedian new rent, Q2 2026
Lisbon municipality17.79 €/m² (highest of the 24 municipalities with more than 100,000 inhabitants; +5.1% year on year, below the national +10.2%)
Greater Lisbon (Grande Lisboa)15.24 €/m²
Portugal10.17 €/m²
 Medians of new contracts reported to the tax authority, in euros per square metre per month. Not asking prices.

A worked example, not an official typical rent: 70 m² × 17.79 €/m² ≈ €1,245 a month in the municipality. The same size at the Greater Lisbon median would be about €1,067; at the national median, about €712.

What this means if you are applying for a visa

The D7 floor for one adult is 100% of the 2026 mainland minimum wage, €920 a month (Decreto-Lei n.º 139/2025; Portaria n.º 1563/2007). In Lisbon, rent at the official median can exceed that floor on its own. That does not automatically refuse a visa, and a consulate may still ask for more than the legal minimum. It does mean you should budget from signed-rent data, not from a hopeful blog figure.

The D8 floor is four times the minimum wage, €3,680 a month. Use the calculator for your household, and read how rental contracts are reported before you sign.

If Lisbon is too tight

The same INE highlight puts the Porto metropolitan area at 10.83 €/m² and lists Greater Lisbon as the most expensive region, followed by Madeira, the Setúbal Peninsula and the Algarve. Porto’s figures and the national cost-of-living overview are the honest comparison. This site is written from the north; that is a vantage point, not a sales pitch.

Quick answers

What is the official median rent in Lisbon in 2026?

INE’s highlight of 29 September 2026 puts the median rent of new contracts in the municipality of Lisbon at 17.79 €/m² in the second quarter of 2026, the highest among the 24 municipalities with more than 100,000 inhabitants. Greater Lisbon as a whole was 15.24 €/m². The national median was 10.17 €/m².

How does that compare with the D7 income rule?

The D7 monthly floor for one adult in 2026 is €920, the mainland minimum wage. A worked example of 70 m² at Lisbon’s official median is about €1,245 a month in rent, which is already above that floor. Your own lease will differ. The D8 floor is €3,680 a month.

Is Greater Lisbon cheaper than the city itself?

Yes, on the official Q2 2026 medians: 15.24 €/m² for Grande Lisboa against 17.79 €/m² for the municipality of Lisbon. Neighbouring municipalities are listed in INE’s accompanying tables.
