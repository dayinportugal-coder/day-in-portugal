Porto · a bed

Sleep where you can hear yourself.

  • Ribeira — one night if you want to step out onto the postcard. After 11pm it is a bar street. Book a room off the front row.
  • Cedofeita or Bonfim — still walking distance, quieter, usually a better rate. This is where we would sleep.
  • Vila Nova de Gaia — the view back at Porto, cellars downstairs, a bridge walk home.

Search those neighbourhoods, not “best hotel Porto”. Filter by cancellation and walking time to São Bento or a metro stop.
