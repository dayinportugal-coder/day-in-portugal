Douro Valley · day trip from Porto

Douro Valley day trip from Porto: train, tour or car.

The short six-bridges boat in Gaia is a city sightseeing loop. The valley is Régua, Pinhão and the terraces — about two hours inland. It is a long day; go if you like wine or landscape, skip it if you have only two days in Porto.

Three ways to do it

Train, tour or car

1. Train to Régua or Pinhão. Leave from São Bento or Campanhã. Sit on the river side. Walk, eat, one booked quinta, train back. Usually the cheapest option — the timetable is thin, so check CP first.

2. Organised day trip. Pickup, viewpoints, two wineries, lunch, a short boat at Pinhão, back in the evening. Nobody has to drive after the tastings.

3. Hire car and the N222. The postcard road between Régua and Pinhão — tight and slow. Park once, and someone stays sober. Read whether a Porto hire car is worth it first.

Good to know

When: late September into October is the harvest — busiest and most beautiful. Spring is green and quieter. July and August are hot inland.

Bring: water, sun cover, shoes for steep gravel. Buy the bottle at the quinta you liked.
