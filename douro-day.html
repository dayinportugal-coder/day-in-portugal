Douro Valley day trip from Porto: tour, train or car

Gifts from Portugal — see the shop

The short six-bridges boat in Gaia is a city sightseeing loop. The valley is Régua, Pinhão and the terraces — about two hours inland. It is a long day; go if you like wine or landscape, skip it if you have only two days in Porto.

Quick answer

  • Easiest: a full-day Douro Valley tour from Porto, about 9–10 hours, two wineries, lunch and a short boat in Pinhão — from about €85 on partner pages (checked 24/10/2026).
  • Cheapest: CP Douro line to Régua and Pinhão. On the timetable valid from 3/11/2026 it takes about 4 hours with a replacement bus and a change at Régua — e.g. São Bento 09:25 → Pinhão 13:27 (CP, checked 13/11/2026).
  • Works on the line: since 3 November 2026 CP runs a replacement bus on part of Porto–Régua (announced until 24 March 2027); Régua–Pinhão–Pocinho is still by train.

Three ways to do it

Train, tour or car

1. Train to Régua or Pinhão. Leave from São Bento or Campanhã. Sit on the river side. Walk, eat, one booked quinta, train back. Usually the cheapest option — the timetable is thin, so check CP first. Since 3 November 2026 part of Porto–Régua runs as a replacement bus, with a change at Régua: São Bento 09:25 reaches Pinhão at 13:27 on the CP timetable (checked 13/11/2026). Allow about four hours each way.

2. Organised day trip. Pickup, viewpoints, two wineries, lunch, a short boat at Pinhão, back in the evening. Nobody has to drive after the tastings.

3. Hire car and the N222. The postcard road between Régua and Pinhão — tight and slow. Park once, and someone stays sober. Read whether a Porto hire car is worth it first.

Frequently asked questions

Is the six-bridges cruise the same as a Douro Valley day trip?

No. The six-bridges cruise is a short harbour loop in Porto and Gaia. A Douro Valley day trip goes about two hours inland to Régua, Pinhão and the terraced vineyards.

What is the best way to do a Douro Valley day trip from Porto?

For most visitors an organised day trip: pickup in Porto, two wineries with tastings, lunch and a boat in Pinhão, and nobody has to drive after the wine. The train is cheaper; a car is the most flexible but someone stays sober.

What is the cheapest Douro day trip from Porto?

The CP train from Porto São Bento or Campanhã to Régua or Pinhão. Between 3 November 2026 and 24 March 2027 part of the line, Marco de Canaveses to Régua, runs as replacement buses, so allow extra time and check cp.pt.

How long does the train to Pinhão take?

About four hours on the timetable valid from 3 November 2026, which includes a replacement bus and a change at Régua: for example São Bento 09:25 → Pinhão 13:27, or Campanhã 07:30 → Pinhão 11:31 (CP, checked 13/11/2026). Re-check on the day.

How long is a Douro Valley day tour?

Organised tours from Porto leave in the morning and are back in the evening — a long day, usually 9 to 10 hours on partner listings.

When is the best time to visit the Douro Valley?

Late September into October for the harvest — busiest and most beautiful. Spring is green and quieter; July and August are hot inland.
