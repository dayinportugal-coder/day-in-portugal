Douro Valley day trip from Porto: train, tour or car.

The short six-bridges boat in Gaia is a city sightseeing loop. The valley is Régua, Pinhão and the terraces.

1. Train from Porto to Régua or Pinhão

Leave from São Bento or Campanhã. Sit on the river side. Walk, eat, one booked quinta, train back. Usually the cheapest option.

2. Organised Douro day trip from Porto

Pickup, viewpoints, lunch, a short boat on the valley river, back in the evening.
